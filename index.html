    Rules that make it stick

    70% is a pass

    Most boxes, not every box. A 5-of-7 day still counts for your streak.

    Never miss twice

    One bad day is normal. After one, do at least the walk the next day.

    Don't rush a phase

    If a phase ends under 60%, move your start date back a week and repeat it.

    Attach to anchors

    Water after brushing, walk after lunch, sun on the way to the canteen.

    Medicines are your doctor's call

    The supplements box is for whatever your doctor prescribes. Don't self-dose high vitamin D.
